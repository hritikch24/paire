## Earlier recommendation
Keep "Muse" for the test group, but pick the final public name before uploading to Play. The application ID (`com.muse.app`, set in `apps/muse-mobile/app.config.js`) cannot change for an existing listing. The display name can be changed without changing that ID; only an ID change requires a new listing.

## Why "Muse" is risky
**What works:** short, easy to say in Hindi and English, fits the tagline "There's a muse for everyone."

**Problems:**
1. **Another dating app on Play is already called "Muse":** ["Muse – Dating App for Lesbian & Queer Women"](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.app.muse_dating_app&hl=en_US) (Black Cherie Technologies). There's also ["Muse: Music Dating"](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/muse-music-dating-social-app/id6756826566) on the App Store. Same name in the same category means trademark risk and users installing the wrong app.
2. **Crowded search.** ["Muse from Meta"](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.facebook.aura&hl=en_IN) (an AI app), the Muse meditation headband and the band Muse all compete for the word, so people who hear about the app from an influencer may not find it.
3. **Existing trademarks.** InteraXon (the headband maker) [holds "MUSE" trademarks](https://choosemuse.com/pages/legal) in the US, Canada and Europe, which cover apps. It's a different business from dating, but it's a risk for a US launch. Get a professional trademark search.

## Candidates
| Name | Why it works | Watch out for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Saath** (साथ, "together") | Fits Duos and safety; warm and Indian; easy enough in the US | May feel too Indian for a strong US push |
| **Clik** | Matches "AI that finds people you'll click with"; global | Similar names may exist |
| **Milan** (मिलन, "meeting") | Romantic and familiar in India | Common first name and a city: crowded search |
| **Jodi** ("pair") | Perfect for Duos and couples | Close to matrimony brands such as Jodi365 |
| **Musely / Muzo** | Keeps the Muse logo idea and tagline | Musely is a skincare app; needs a different spelling |

Leaning: **Saath** if India is the main market, **Clik** for equal India/US appeal.

## Before choosing
Check each shortlisted name across Play and App Store listings, domains, and the Indian trademark registry (classes 9, 42 and 45). Then update the app name, application ID, wordmark (`tools/generate_brand_assets.py`) and legal pages in one change.

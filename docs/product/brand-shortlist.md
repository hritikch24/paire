### New collision findings change the earlier shortlist

- **Aarzo:** downgrade from romantic finalist. [Aarzoo's own website](https://www.rzoo.org/) and [company profile](https://in.linkedin.com/company/weareaarzoo) describe an Indian LGBTQ+ matchmaking service. A separate [Aarzoo relationship waitlist](https://joinaarzoo.com/) also surfaced. These are similar rather than identical spellings; potential confusion is our inference. Changing a vowel does not give us a clearly distinct identity.
- **Roohio / Rooha:** drop from the lead shortlist. [Rooh](https://rooh.dating/) already presents an upcoming dating service and [Rooh AI](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.cplock.rooh_ai) describes a virtual romantic companion. Shared pronunciation/root creates a plausible confusion risk, even though the candidate spellings differ.
- **Duet** and **Kindred:** direct dating-category use exists ([Duet](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=next.bondee.tech), [Kindred](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=app.kindred.dating)).
- **Raabta:** already used by [Raabta Social](https://apps.apple.com/gr/app/raabta-social/id6444019910), as well as other software businesses. Do not label it an unused name or claim the social app is a dating app.

This search covered exact-name dating queries, broader software/brand use, selected spelling/root variants, and indexed official store listings. It is not a complete trademark search. Negative search results do not establish availability. Some primary pages block direct fetching; supporting indexed extracts were available. No domain availability or price is claimed.

## Search scope and limits

Queries used exact spellings with “dating”, “matrimony”, “app” and broader brand terms, plus searches restricted to Google Play and Apple App Store indexed pages. Commercial facts above link to the brands' own sites or official store listings. Search engines can omit new, unindexed, regional or removed apps; a negative search result is not evidence of availability. Phonetic, Hindi/Urdu-script and similar-name searches remain incomplete.

The working name has a direct category collision: the [official Google Play Muse listing](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.app.muse_dating_app&hl=en_US) describes a dating app for queer women. That supports choosing a distinct public identity, while preserving the founder's instruction to keep Muse until a decision.

**No domains or handles were checked for availability, bought or reserved.** Candidate patterns such as `join<name>` or `<name>.in` are ideas only. Do not publish an invented `support@<name>` address, put an unowned domain in legal documents, or purchase a domain based on this review.

Before choosing, run a small adult-user name test in NCR and outside North India: hear it once, spell it, recall it later, and describe the expected product. Then use the [official IP India trademark search](https://tmrsearch.ipindia.gov.in/tmrpublicsearch), with a qualified reviewer deciding the relevant classes and checking similar sounds/scripts; also check current direct store listings, registrars and handles. No trademark search was completed in this review. Domain registration alone does not establish trademark rights. Once the founder chooses and the brand is cleared, update display names, web copy, support/legal details and marketing together; existing native application IDs need not change merely for a display-name change.

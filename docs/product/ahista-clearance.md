# Ahista: preliminary legal screen and tagline drafts

Reviewed **11 October 2026** following the founder's question about legal use. **Not cleared. Do not treat the earlier creative recommendation as permission to launch or a finding of exclusive availability. Muse remains unchanged.**

## Material finding

[IndiaFilings reports AAHISTA, application 4735623](https://www.indiafilings.com/search/aahista-tm-4735623), as a registered word mark in Class 45, proprietor Ratin Chhibber, application date 6 November 2020. The listed specification covers legal/security services and personal/social services meeting individuals' needs. This is a **third-party record**, whose own disclaimer says information may be incomplete or outdated; current official status and the actual registered specification have not been confirmed.

[WIPO's 2026 Nice Classification, Class 45](https://nclpub.wipo.int/esen/pdf-download.pdf?classNumber=45&dateInForce=20260101&lang=en&viewMode=flat) includes dating and online social networking services. Ahista and Aahista are similar-sounding spellings. Taken together, these facts are a material reason to obtain professional clearance rather than adopting the name now. The inference is potential conflict, **not** a conclusion that registration or use is automatically prohibited: class numbers alone do not establish infringement or the precise coverage of an older registration.

Indian trademark registration considers similarity of marks, goods/services and likelihood of public confusion under [section 11 of the Trade Marks Act, 1999](https://upload.indiacode.nic.in/view-casepdf?id=AC_CEN_11_60_00004_199947_1517807323972&type=act). A spelling change or obtaining a domain is not a substitute for reviewing that risk.

The [official IP India public search](https://tmrsearch.ipindia.gov.in/tmrpublicsearch/) now requires OTP login and a CAPTCHA. The [AI trademark search](https://tmsearch.ipindia.gov.in/) also leads to a login. Neither was bypassed. A complete official wordmark, phonetic, Hindi-script and similar-name search remains outstanding. A qualified Indian trademark reviewer should confirm application 4735623 and examine the relevant dating, downloadable-app and software services, commonly involving Classes 45, 9 and 42 as appropriate to the actual product.

## Apps and other use

No obvious exact-name dating product surfaced in this bounded search, including dating queries restricted to indexed Google Play and Apple App Store pages. This does not prove that none exists: search indexes omit listings, regional services and unlaunched products. It also does not establish trademark availability.

Related existing use includes the [Aahista Aahista novel-reader app](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.aarishapps.aahistaaahista.asiaraeeskhan) and [Ahista Tea](https://ahistatea.com/policies/terms-of-service), whose own terms state that its mark is registered. IndiaFilings also lists [AHISTA in Class 5, application 1637957](https://www.indiafilings.com/search/ahista-tm-1637957); that secondary record is not an independently confirmed official registration. These other categories are not by themselves decisive for dating services. The reported Class 45 AAHISTA record is the main concern found here.

No domain/handle was bought or reserved. No formal opinion, registrar availability confirmation, complete trademark search or filing was obtained.

## Tagline drafts, conditional on clearing the brand

Preferred acquisition headline: **“Your next hello could mean more.”** It invites someone to try a conversation without promising a match or a relationship. This is a creative recommendation; user conversion and distinctiveness have not been measured.

- Short brand signature: **“Your pace. Your person.”**
- Hindi/Hinglish campaign: **“Ek hello. Nayi kahaani.”**
- Romantic alternative: **“A little hello. A beautiful maybe.”**
- Broad alternative: **“Your kind of connection.”**

The initial draft “Meet someone worth slowing down for” was rejected after the exact sentence surfaced on [Master Matchmakers' Florida page](https://www.mastermatchmakers.com/florida-matchmaker). No obvious exact-phrase brand use surfaced for the new preferred headline in a bounded indexed search; this does not establish exclusive rights or legal clearance.

Do not claim guaranteed matches, complete safety or universal verified membership. Tagline legal clearance is also outstanding. Compare the invitation headline with the shorter signature in adult-user recall tests before paid marketing.

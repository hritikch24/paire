# Wedding Nods: what marketing may say

*"Shaadi mein ek nod."* Use only the claims on this page. If a claim is not here, don't make it; ask the owner. Source of truth: [`docs/wedding-nods.md`](../wedding-nods.md) and the page https://www.paire.in/wedding/.

## True, and allowed

**What it is**
- Wedding Nods is a feature in the Paire browser app (www.paire.in/app). It is Paire's own feature: we built it ourselves and it is not a partnership with any wedding planner, venue, host or brand.
- Adults going to the same wedding can give each other a private "nod". If both nod, they match. If only one does, nobody is told.
- It is found in the app under Profile → "Going to a wedding?". It does not appear on the Discover screen or the homepage.
- Free to use. (It uses no likes and no Paire Plus. Chatting after a match follows Paire's normal messaging rules.)

**How it works**
- You add a wedding by typing the bride's first name, the groom's first name, the wedding date and the venue's PIN code. Guests who type the same details land in the same wedding, even with a one-letter typo in a name.
- Weddings can't be listed or searched. Nobody can browse weddings.
- You choose the bride's side or the groom's side and write a short "spot me" clue, like "navy sherwani near the stage". You get an anonymous nickname.
- Other guests see only a nickname, a side, an age band (18-24, 25-29, 30-34, 35+) and your clue. No photo, real name, phone number, link or location.
- You get 3 nods per wedding.
- A nod is secret. The other person is not told, and nobody can see who nodded them.
- A mutual nod creates a normal Paire match and the usual "It's a match" notification. Chat then works like any Paire chat.
- Your usual Paire preferences apply: your gender and age choices, distance, blocks and privacy settings. If you've blocked someone, you won't see them.
- Until enough guests have joined, the app shows "Guests are still joining" instead of any guests.
- You can report or block anyone from their card. Reporting also blocks. Several different reports hide a guest from that wedding.
- You can leave a wedding any time.

**People and privacy**
- 18+ only. Members need a confirmed email and to pass Paire's photo check, the same as for Discover.
- Attendance is self-declared. Paire does not verify that anyone is at the wedding.
- There is no group chat, no public room and no guest list. There is no host: the couple and families are not involved and can't see anything.
- Paire does not store your location for this feature.
- An optional photo of the invitation can be added. It is private to Paire's safety team, never shown to other guests, and deleted after 30 days.
- A wedding closes 7 days after its date. Nods, nicknames and clues are then deleted. Matches that already happened stay.
- Deleting your Paire account erases all your wedding data.

## Not true, never say

- That guests are verified, that the wedding is verified, or that "everyone is a real guest".
- That it is "safe", "100% safe" or "risk free", or any safety guarantee. (Safe-use advice is fine: meet in public, tell someone.)
- That you will get a match, or how likely a match is.
- Any number of members, weddings, guests, matches or nods. We have no numbers to share.
- That it works on Android or iPhone apps. It is browser only for now.
- That the bride, groom, host, family or friends can see, approve or are told anything.
- That it works at every wedding or in every city, or that a particular wedding is "on Paire".
- That nods are "anonymous forever". A match reveals the matched profiles in the normal way.
- That we read, scan or review people's chats for this feature beyond Paire's normal chat safety.
- Any claim about specific communities, religions or castes. Paire has no caste field and doesn't use one.

# Paire launch: ads, headlines and video prompts

Site: **www.paire.in** · App: **www.paire.in/app/** · Garba page: **www.paire.in/garba-nights/**
Tagline: **Find your Paire.** · Brand colours: plum `#3A1236`, rose `#A43754`, coral `#EE7A82`, marigold `#F6C86B`, paper `#FAF6F0`.

## Before the first ad (do these first)

1. **Meta needs written approval for dating ads.** In Meta Business Suite, apply for the "Online dating" permission (Ads Manager → Help → "Dating ads" request, or ask Meta support for the dating advertiser application). Ads for dating services run only after approval. Same idea on Google Ads ("Dating and companionship" policy).
2. **Pixel:** create a Meta Pixel in Events Manager, then add its ID to Vercel as `PAIRE_META_PIXEL_ID` and redeploy. The site then reports `PageView`, `Lead` (opened the app), `CompleteRegistration` (finished sign-up) and `Purchase` (Plus pass, with the amount). Optimise campaigns for `CompleteRegistration`.
3. **Targeting rules for dating ads:** 18+ only (use 21–35 to start), India, Delhi NCR first. Never target or imply sexual orientation, and don't use "single?"-style wording that asserts personal attributes ("Are you single?" is not allowed by Meta). Say what Paire does instead.
4. **Creative rules:** no sexual or suggestive content, no real people's likeness without written consent, everyone shown must look clearly 25+, no fake "she messaged you" notifications, no claims of guaranteed matches.

## Video prompts (AI video tools)

Use these in Veo, Sora, Kling, Runway, Pika, Hailuo/MiniMax or Meta AI. Add your logo and captions in an editor afterwards (CapCut, Instagram Edits): AI tools garble text, so ask for **no on-screen text** and overlay it yourself. Always say "adults in their late 20s" and "no real person's likeness".

Close-up of the phone screen glowing pink (no readable text). A small paper aeroplane made of pale pink paper, with a heart-shaped cut-out, lifts off the screen and glides through the café, past fairy lights and chai glasses, landing gently on the table of a man in his late 20s reading a book across the room. He unfolds it, looks up, and they share a shy smile. Soft bokeh, plum and rose colour grade, gentle handheld camera, shallow depth of field, 24fps film look, no on-screen text, no logos, fictional people only.

**Overlay:** 0–3 s "No more 'hi'." · 4–9 s "On Paire, every like comes with a note." · 10–15 s logo + "Find your Paire. www.paire.in"

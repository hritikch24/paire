# Site review, 17 October 2026

A full pass through www.paire.in's browser app as a new member and as members with matches, run on **local emulators with fictional accounts only** (no production accounts, as `CLAUDE.md` requires). The live site itself couldn't be reached from the review environment, so the marketing pages were checked from a local build.

## What was checked

- **New member:** welcome → sign up → "Check your inbox" → basics (name, birthday, gender, show me) → photo (skipped) → interests → Discover. Smooth, clear copy, no errors.
- **Discover:** People, Vibe first, Duos, filters, like/pass/note/Super Like, the match moment ("It's a match!"), daily likes counter.
- **Matches and chat:** Liked you row, conversation list, sending a message, the chat menu (view profile, close kindly, unmatch, report, block).
- **Profile and Paire Plus screens**, at phone (320–430 px) and desktop (1440 px) widths.
- Existing browser regressions `e2e/discovery-layout.cjs` and `e2e/discovery-empty.cjs`.

## Issues found and fixed

| Issue | Fix |
|---|---|
| Paire Plus screen said **"4× the likes"**, but it's 100 vs 10 a day | The multiplier is now worked out from the real limits ("10× the likes"). |
| `README.md` said free members get **25** likes a day | Now 10, matching the app, site and server. |
| Discover header cut off "Starting in Delhi NCR · open to all" for members without a city | Shortened to "Delhi NCR · open to all". |
| The empty-Discover browser test still expected the old "all caught up" screen after free members started seeing the Paire Plus offer | The offer keeps the "Expand search" / "Check again" action and its description; the test checks the offer. |

**Seen, not changed:** React warns in development about `accessibilityState` and `autoCorrect={false}` reaching the page from shared button and input components. It doesn't affect members, and those components are shared with the native app, so they were left alone.

## New features

- **Hold to Super Like** (web Discover). Tap the heart to like; press and hold it and a blue ring fills around it, the heart turns into a star, and on release of a full ring it's sent as a Super Like with a little burst. Letting go early is a normal like. Keyboard users still get a plain like; the tooltip explains the hold.
- **Lucky spin** (web Discover footer). "🎲 Lucky spin" spins through your cards like a slot machine and lands on one person, whose card comes to the top. Names and ages only, never photos (some members keep photos private until a match), and nothing is sent: it only reorders the cards you already have.
- **Dating vibe quiz** at `/vibe-quiz/`, for bringing in new people. Six quick questions → Cozy Romantic, Midnight Philosopher, Adventure Spark or Movie Dreamer, with a perfect first date, a green flag and the vibe you click with. Share to WhatsApp or copy a link (`/vibe-quiz/?r=…` shows a friend's result with "Take the quiz"). No sign-up, no backend: answers never leave the browser. Linked from the homepage menu, in the sitemap and `llms.txt`.

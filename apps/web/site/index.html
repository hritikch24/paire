06 / STRAIGHT ANSWERS

Questions, answered.

Is Paire free?

Joining, matching and safety are free. Everyone gets 10 likes a day with notes, 1 Super Like a day, every privacy and safety control, and can see an anonymous likes count and read every message they receive, free. Paire Plus reveals who liked you. To send messages you need your own Paire Plus pass (₹399 for 60 days with the month offer, or ₹35 for a day; with more likes too), Lifetime Plus for ₹1,499 once with no expiry, or Unlimited Chat: ₹49 once for all your current and future matches, with no expiry. Nothing auto-renews, and men and women pay the same.

Is Paire safe for women?

Paire is built around women's control. Turn on notes-only likes so nobody can like you without saying why, choose “I'll message first”, go incognito so only people you like can see you, or keep your photos private until you match. Every chat has report and block, and Paire AI warns you about money, OTP and scam messages. All of it is free.

How is Paire different from Tinder, Bumble or Hinge?

On Paire every like can carry a note, so the first message is never just “hi”. Paire Plus reveals who likes you, with passes that never auto-renew. Women get free controls like notes-only likes and incognito. Passes start at ₹35 and never auto-renew. And it is built for India: languages, height, diet and other basics, and never a caste filter.

Is Paire for casual dating or marriage?

Both. You choose what you're looking for: marriage-minded, long-term, something short-term, new friends or still figuring it out. Turn on “same intentions only” and you'll only see people who want the same thing. Your search filters control who you see, not who can see you.

Does Paire have caste filters?

No, never. Religion is optional and shown only if you add it, and Paire never uses it to filter or rank anyone.

What is Diwali Together on Paire?

Diwali Together is a voluntary Diwali 2026 edition of Paire for adults away from home, running 24 October to 10 November 2026 (India time). You choose up to three days and three simple public plans, and see members nearby who chose a matching day and plan. You only match when you both like each other. Paire does not host events or guarantee meet-ups. Joining and matching are free; sending messages works as it does on Paire today. Details are on the Diwali Together page.

Which cities is Paire in?

Paire is open to men and women across India, in every city and town.

Do I need to download an app?

No. Paire works in your phone or laptop browser at paire.in/app, with Google or email sign-in. The Android app is coming soon.

Who can join Paire?

Men and women aged 18 and over. Profiles are reviewed, reported accounts are hidden quickly, and accounts that offer or ask for money are removed.

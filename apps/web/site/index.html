06 / STRAIGHT ANSWERS

Questions, answered.

Is Paire free?

Yes. Everyone gets 25 likes a day with notes, 1 Super Like a day, unlimited chat, every privacy and safety control, and can see everyone who likes them, free. Paire Plus is an optional pass for more reach: ₹20 for a day, ₹99 for a week or ₹299 for a month. It never auto-renews, and men and women pay the same.

Is Paire safe for women?

Paire is built around women's control. Turn on notes-only likes so nobody can like you without saying why, choose “I'll message first”, go incognito so only people you like can see you, or keep your photos private until you match. Every chat has report and block, and Paire AI warns you about money, OTP and scam messages. All of it is free.

How is Paire different from Tinder, Bumble or Hinge?

On Paire every like can carry a note, so the first message is never just “hi”. Seeing who likes you is free, while Tinder and Bumble charge for it. Women get free controls like notes-only likes and incognito. Passes start at ₹20 and never auto-renew. And it is built for India: languages, height, diet and other basics, and never a caste filter.

Is Paire for casual dating or marriage?

Both. You choose what you're looking for: marriage-minded, long-term, something short-term, new friends or still figuring it out. Turn on “same intentions only” and you'll only see, and be seen by, people who want the same thing.

Does Paire have caste filters?

No, never. Religion is optional and shown only if you add it, and Paire never uses it to filter or rank anyone.

What is Garba Nights on Paire?

A free Navratri edition of Paire. Pick up to four nights you're going to Garba or Dandiya and meet people near you going on the same nights. Nobody sees which nights you picked, only how many you share. Paire AI can even suggest a Garba-themed opener once you match.

Which cities is Paire in?

Paire starts in Delhi NCR (Delhi, Noida, Gurugram, Faridabad and Ghaziabad) and is open to men and women across India.

Do I need to download an app?

No. Paire works in your phone or laptop browser at paire.in/app, with Google or email sign-in. The Android app is coming soon.

Who can join Paire?

Men and women aged 18 and over. Profiles are reviewed, reported accounts are hidden quickly, and accounts that offer or ask for money are removed.

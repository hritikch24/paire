GARBA NIGHTS · QUESTIONS

Before the first beat.

How do I find a Garba or Dandiya partner on Paire?

Open Paire, tap “🪔 Garba Nights”, pick up to four nights you're going this Navratri, and you'll see people near you going on the same nights. Like them with a note, match, and plan to meet in the circle.

Is Garba Nights free?

Yes. Garba Nights is free for everyone during Navratri, 11–20 October 2026.

Can people see which nights I'm going?

No. Others only see how many of your nights they share, never which ones. Once you match, Paire AI may suggest an opener about a night you both picked.

Does Garba Nights need my location?

Yes, your approximate location, so it only shows people within about 50 km who could actually be at the same Garba. Your exact location is never shown.

Is it safe to meet someone at a Garba?

Meet in the crowd, go with your group, and share your pass or venue only once you're comfortable. Paire has report and block in every chat and warns you about scam messages.

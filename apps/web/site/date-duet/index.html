A starting point, not a promise

This isn't a compatibility test or a Paire match. It doesn't contact the other person, book a venue or set a date. Both people still decide whether to meet. Links aren't identity-verified and can be edited by whoever holds them.

Choose a public place together, check costs and accessibility directly, and use your own transport. Never put contact details, a venue or an exact schedule in a Duet.

What happens to our answers?

Only preset choices are used. No names, profiles, messages or exact location are collected by Date Duet. A share link keeps choices after the # in the address. They aren't sent in the page request or our visit counter. Anyone who receives the link can read, edit and forward those choices. Use “Start fresh” to clear the choices on this page; copies already shared will still work.

Is this free, and do we need to match?

Date Duet is free and doesn't need an account. It creates a planning prompt, not a match or permission to contact a member. Paire's normal mutual-match, verification, messaging and payment rules apply in the app.

What if we choose different things?

We show what needs a conversation. We never replace someone's comfort preference, raise their spending cap or invent a shared time window to force a plan.

Copyright infringement notice

If you believe content on Paire infringes your copyright, email [GRIEVANCE EMAIL] (subject: "Copyright notice") or write to the postal address on our contact page. Please include:

  1. your name, postal address, phone number and email;
  2. a description of the copyrighted work you say is infringed;
  3. where the content is on Paire (the member's name and what appears, or a screenshot), so we can find it;
  4. a statement that you have a good-faith belief that the use isn't authorized by the copyright owner, its agent or the law;
  5. a statement that the information in your notice is accurate and that you are the copyright owner or authorized to act for the owner; and
  6. your physical or electronic signature.

We handle these notices under India's Copyright Act, 1957 and IT Rules, 2021. We remove or disable access to the content promptly, tell the member who posted it, and terminate the accounts of repeat infringers in appropriate circumstances. Knowingly false notices can lead to liability for damages.

Counter-notice

If your content was removed and you believe it was a mistake or you have the right to use it, send a counter-notice to the same address with: your name, address, phone number and email; what was removed and where it appeared; a statement that you have a good-faith belief it was removed by mistake or misidentification; and your signature. Unless the original complainant tells us they have filed a court action, we may restore the content 10–14 business days after receiving a valid counter-notice.

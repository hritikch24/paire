Your privacy choices

Paire never sells your personal data and never shows third-party ads.

Advertising measurement

When we run ads, we'd like to use the Meta Pixel on our website to see whether those ads bring sign-ups and purchases. It receives the pages you visit on our site and whether you signed up or bought a pass. It never receives your profile, photos, messages, matches or preferences. Some US state laws, such as California's, treat this as "sharing" personal information for advertising, so it stays off unless you allow it.

Checking your choice…

This choice is saved in this browser. If you use Paire in another browser or device, set it there too. We also honour the Global Privacy Control (GPC) signal: if your browser sends it, advertising measurement stays off whatever is chosen here.

Your other rights

You can access, correct, download or delete your data, and withdraw consent for anything you agreed to. See your rights where you live, delete your account in the app or on the account deletion page, or email [SUPPORT EMAIL]. US residents can also send requests through an authorized agent.

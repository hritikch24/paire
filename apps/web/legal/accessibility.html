Our commitment

We want everyone to be able to use Paire, including people who use screen readers, keyboard navigation, magnification or other assistive technology. We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at level AA on our website and web app.

What we do

  • Buttons and controls have text labels for screen readers, and actions on swipe cards can also be done with buttons and the keyboard.
  • Text and controls are sized for touch, layouts work from small phones to large screens, and the page can be zoomed.
  • Our newer animations respect your device's "reduce motion" setting, and we're extending this to the rest of the app.
  • Light and dark themes, with colours chosen for contrast.

Known limitations

Photos uploaded by members don't always have descriptions, and some newer features may not yet be fully accessible. We test regularly and fix problems as we find them.

Tell us about a barrier

If something on Paire is hard or impossible for you to use, email [SUPPORT EMAIL] with the page or feature and the assistive technology you use. We reply within 5 working days and will help you get what you need in another way while we fix it.

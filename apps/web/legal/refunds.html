Cancellation and Refund Policy

Effective [EFFECTIVE DATE]. Applies to Paire Plus passes bought on the Paire website through Razorpay. Passes bought in the Android app follow Google Play's refund process.

What you buy

Paire Plus is a digital pass for a fixed period: 1 day (₹20), 7 days (₹99) or 30 days (₹299), inclusive of applicable taxes. It is a one-time payment and never renews automatically, so there is nothing to cancel: it simply ends.

Delivery

The pass is a digital service delivered to your Paire account as soon as your payment is confirmed, usually within a minute and at most a few hours if your bank is slow to confirm. Nothing is shipped. If your pass hasn't started within 24 hours of a successful payment, contact us and we'll activate it or refund you in full.

Refunds

  • Charged but no pass, double charged, or charged the wrong amount: full refund.
  • Changed your mind: ask within 24 hours of buying and before you've used any of the pass's extra likes or Super Likes, and we'll refund you in full.
  • Account removed by us for breaking our Terms or Community Guidelines: no refund for the remaining time.
  • Otherwise, a pass that has started isn't refundable for the unused time, because its benefits are available immediately.

Paying for Plus never guarantees a match or a reply, and that is not a reason for a refund.

A refunded pass (in full or in part) ends as soon as the refund is processed.

How to ask

Email [SUPPORT EMAIL] from your account's email address with the Razorpay payment ID (it's in the receipt Razorpay emails you). We reply within 2 working days. Approved refunds go back to your original payment method within 5–7 working days, depending on your bank.

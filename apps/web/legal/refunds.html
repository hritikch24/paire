Cancellation and Refund Policy

Effective [EFFECTIVE DATE]. Applies to Paire Plus passes and the Unlimited Chat unlock bought on the Paire website and paid through CCAvenue, and to earlier purchases paid by UPI directly to Paire's own account (paire.in@ybl) or through Razorpay. Passes bought in the Android app follow Google Play's refund process.

What you buy

Paire Plus is a digital pass for a fixed period: 1 day (₹20), 7 days (₹119, or ₹99 for members whose photos are verified when they buy) or 1 month (₹299), inclusive of applicable taxes. A 1-month pass bought on the website currently includes a second month free (60 days in all); the free month is part of the same pass and has no separate value. It is a one-time payment and never renews automatically, so there is nothing to cancel: it simply ends.

Unlimited Chat is a one-time ₹49 payment, inclusive of applicable taxes, that lets your account send messages in all your current and future mutual matches. It has no expiry and never renews, so there is nothing to cancel, and it is never sold to you twice.

Delivery

Passes and Unlimited Chat are digital services delivered to your Paire account as soon as your payment is confirmed, usually within a minute and at most a few hours if your bank is slow to confirm. Nothing is shipped. If your pass hasn't started, or chat hasn't unlocked, within 24 hours of a successful payment, contact us and we'll activate it or refund you in full.

A pass or Unlimited Chat starts as soon as CCAvenue confirms the payment, usually within seconds (a few minutes if your bank is slow to confirm). If you're charged twice for the same thing, or we can't match a payment to an order, we refund it to your original payment method automatically. (An earlier payment made by UPI directly to Paire's account started once we confirmed it arrived.)

Refunds

  • Charged but no pass or unlock, double charged, or charged the wrong amount: full refund. A second payment for Unlimited Chat you already own is refunded automatically.
  • Changed your mind: ask within 24 hours of buying and before you've used any of the pass's extra likes or Super Likes (or, for Unlimited Chat, before you've sent a message with it), and we'll refund you in full.
  • Account removed by us for breaking our Terms or Community Guidelines: no refund for the remaining time.
  • Otherwise, a pass that has started isn't refundable for the unused time, because its benefits are available immediately.
  • United Kingdom and European Union: you have a legal right to cancel a purchase within 14 days of buying it. Email us within 14 days and we'll refund you in full.

Nothing in this policy limits refunds or remedies you're entitled to under the consumer laws where you live, including India's Consumer Protection Act, 2019, and if a pass or unlock doesn't work as described, we'll fix it or refund you.

Paying for Plus or Unlimited Chat never guarantees a match or a reply, and that is not a reason for a refund.

A refunded pass (in full or in part) ends as soon as the refund is processed. A refunded or charged-back Unlimited Chat unlock ends the same way; it never affects a separate Plus pass, and a Plus pass ending never affects Unlimited Chat.

How to ask

Email [SUPPORT EMAIL] from your account's email address with the CCAvenue order number or tracking ID from your payment receipt (or, for an earlier payment, the 12-digit UPI reference (UTR) or the Razorpay payment ID). We reply within 2 working days. Approved refunds go back to your original payment method within 5–7 working days, depending on your bank.

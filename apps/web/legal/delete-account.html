Delete your Paire account

Paire is published on Google Play by Hritik Chaudhary. You can delete your account and data at any time.

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Paire and go to the Profile tab.
  2. Tap Settings, then Delete account.
  3. Confirm with your password. Deletion happens immediately and you are signed out.

Without the app

Email [SUPPORT EMAIL] from the email address on your account with the subject "Delete my Paire account". We will verify the request and delete the account within 7 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your sign-in account, profile, photos and private details (date of birth, preferences, location)
  • Your likes and passes, likes you received, your matches and all messages in them
  • Your blocks and push notification tokens

What we keep, and for how long

Safety reports you made or that were made about you, and related moderation records, may be kept for up to [RETENTION PERIOD, e.g. 3 years] to protect members and meet legal obligations. Encrypted backups are overwritten within 30 days.

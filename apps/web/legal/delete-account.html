Delete your Paire account

Paire is published on Google Play by Hritik Chaudhary. You can delete your account and data at any time.

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Paire and go to the Profile tab.
  2. Tap Settings, then Delete account.
  3. Confirm with your password. Deletion happens immediately and you are signed out.

Without the app

Email [SUPPORT EMAIL] from the email address on your account with the subject "Delete my Paire account". We verify the request to protect your account and handle it within applicable legal deadlines. We explain any data that must be retained.

What gets deleted

  • Your sign-in account, profile, photos and private details (date of birth, preferences, location)
  • Your likes and passes, likes you received, your matches and all messages in them
  • Your blocks, browser push subscriptions and notification tokens, and non-financial Plus gift requests you sent or received

What we keep, and for how long

Safety reports you made or that were made about you, and related moderation records, may be kept for up to 3 years where needed; a legal hold or applicable preservation duty may require longer retention to protect members and meet legal obligations. Backup expiry follows provider controls and any legal hold. Financial order, payment and refund records are separate and retained as explained in our Privacy Policy.
